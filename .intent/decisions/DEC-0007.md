## Context

`hooks/useBinderActions.js` imported `MoveBinderSlotDocument` and `RemoveBinderSlotDocument` from `@sixbyfive/tcg-binder`. These operations do not exist in the new tcg-runtime gateway schema. The equivalent operation is `saveBinderPagesAndSlots` (bulk save of page/slot state).

## Decision (resolved 2026-05-17)

The stub aliases and the incorrect `useMutation` hooks have been removed. `moveSlot` and `removeSlot` now build a full pages payload using the same helpers as the rest of the hook (`normalizePositions`, `slotCardId`, etc.) and call `savePagesMut` directly:

- **moveSlot**: removes the slot from the source page, inserts it at the target page/position, bulk-saves.
- **removeSlot**: filters the target slot out of its page, bulk-saves.

This matches the `saveBinderPagesAndSlots(binderId, pages)` variable shape exactly.

## Consequences

- Binder slot moving and removal now work correctly against the new backend.
- No separate per-slot mutation is needed; all binder state changes go through the single bulk-save path.
