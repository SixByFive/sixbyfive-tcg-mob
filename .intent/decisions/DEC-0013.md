## Context

`app/settings.js` rendered all content in a root `View` with no `ScrollView`. On smaller screens or when collapsible sections (Change Password, Change Email, 2FA, Safety) were expanded, content below the fold was unreachable.

Additionally, the `Collapsible` component measured its children's height once at mount and locked the animated container to that height with `overflow: "hidden"`. When dynamic content (e.g., a status/error banner) appeared inside an already-open collapsible, the extra height was clipped and invisible.

## Decision

1. **ScrollView**: Wrapped all settings content in a `ScrollView` between the `PageHeaderBackSimple` header and the closing `View`, with `paddingBottom: 40` and `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`.

2. **Collapsible overflow fix**: Added `fullyOpen` state that becomes `true` when the open animation `.start()` callback fires with `finished: true`. When `fullyOpen`:
   - The `Animated.View` drops the `height` constraint entirely
   - `overflow` switches from `"hidden"` to `"visible"`
   - Dynamic content (banners, new fields) can grow freely

   When closing, `fullyOpen` resets immediately so the height-to-zero animation runs with clipping intact.

## Consequences

- All settings content is reachable by scrolling on any screen size.
- Error and success banners inside expanded sections are always visible.
- The pattern (measure once, animate open, then free the height) is suitable for any other `Collapsible` usage in the app.
